Most "X vs Y" pages are marketing. This one is written under the same honesty
policy as our [accessibility page](/accessibility/): every number comes from a
primary source (npm registry/downloads API, GitHub API, bundlephobia, official
docs and pricing pages), collected **2026-08-10**, and we lead with where we
lose.

<Aside type="caution" title="Read this first">
tekivex-ui is 4 months old, has ~85 npm downloads a week, and zero GitHub
stars. Every library below beats us on adoption by four to six orders of
magnitude. If ecosystem size, hiring familiarity, or a decade of production
battle-testing is what you need most, pick one of them today — this page
exists to tell you precisely when that calculus flips.
</Aside>

## Where the incumbents beat us

- **Trust and track record.** They have years of production edge cases, huge
  communities, Stack Overflow depth, and hiring familiarity. We have none of
  that yet.
- **Accessibility pedigree.** Our APG audit was internal — adversarially
  verified and fully fixed, but internal. [React Aria](https://react-aria.adobe.com)
  is maintained by Adobe's accessibility team and tested against
  VoiceOver/NVDA/JAWS/TalkBack; it is the benchmark our keyboard models chase,
  not a peer we've matched.
- **Ecosystem depth.** MUI X's commercial Data Grid, Mantine's satellite
  packages, Ant Design's free Pro layer, shadcn's template economy — each goes
  deeper in its specialty than our equivalent.
- **Scale of testing.** Ant Design enforces 100% line coverage; our 2,168
  tests are genuine but jsdom-only — no cross-browser grid or screen-reader
  lab yet.

## Where tekivex-ui genuinely wins

We verified each of these against the market before claiming it:

1. **A shipped security kernel — no mainstream equivalent found.** URL
   sanitisation, Trojan-Source/homoglyph defence, PII scrubbing, and a Trusted
   Types installer run at the prop boundary, backed by a
   [published threat model and CycloneDX SBOM](/security/). Elsewhere these
   exist only as app-level guides or standalone CI tools — never inside the
   component library. No competitor on this page publishes a threat model or
   SBOM.
2. **The India-first form suite — unique as an integrated suite.** Validated
   PAN / Voter ID / driving-licence inputs and a PIN-code cascading address
   (Country → State → District → Sub-district). The pieces exist as scattered
   npm utilities; no kit ships them as polished components.
3. **Zero runtime dependencies.** The smallest possible supply-chain surface —
   against 12 (MUI), 48 (antd), 7 (Chakra), 5 (Mantine).
4. **Honest accounting.** We published our own failed accessibility audit —
   35 confirmed APG violations — then fixed all 35 with regression tests and
   kept the original findings public. We believe we're the only styled kit on
   this page that has done that.
5. **One versioned kit with unusual breadth** — charts, realtime, browser-native
   PDF, 44 locales (incl. RTL + Indic scripts), and headless hooks under one
   semver. (Dedicated PDF libraries like @react-pdf/renderer and PDFx do PDFs
   too — our claim is bundling, not uniqueness.)

## Our honest recommendation

- Data-dense enterprise admin, free: **Ant Design** (mind its documented
  accessibility gaps). Commercial support and SLAs: **MUI + MUI X**.
- Batteries-included and free with tiny deps: **Mantine**. Total code
  ownership on Tailwind: **shadcn/ui**. Audit-grade a11y primitives to style
  yourself: **React Aria**.
- **tekivex-ui** when the trade favors what we uniquely have: India-market
  forms, a security/compliance posture you can hand to an auditor (threat
  model, SBOM, zero deps), or one kit covering charts + realtime + PDF + i18n
  without assembling five libraries — and you can accept a young project with
  a small community.
